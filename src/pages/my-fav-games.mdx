<div className="game-div">

my childhood like nothing else has. TreeTopia/CreatureTopia, Jaffa Factory, SipsCo, Hole Diggers, there's so so much to

<div className="game-div">
<IqdbCover game="DOOM" floatRight={true} />
## DOOM / DOOM 2 - A long time
This game transcends time itself, I mean, most old games don't age well, but Doom aged like a fine fucking wine because
the first time I beat Doom 2 was around 2015 and I enjoyed the shit out of it. Everyone can get into Doom. Nobody minds
the graphics. Nobody complains about clunky mechanics. It plays beautifully. I also played a ton of multiplayer on
Zandronum with the widest variety of people - exes, friends from ages ago, friends from not long ago - and I love the
modding community around it (I think it's also the oldest game to ever have mods!), trans rights! ❤️ Romero

followed the devblog from the earliest stages of development up until release, back when I was little, which can be said
about practically no other game I've been into. These games are special!
</div>
